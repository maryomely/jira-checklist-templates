# Business Travel Preparation ✈️
## Travel Details
-! Trip dates and destination are **confirmed**
- Travel purpose and agenda are documented
- Traveler details are verified
-! Travel budget is approved by @Ops_Manager
- Passport and visa requirements are checked
- Company travel policy is reviewed – [📖 Travel Policy](travel_policy_url)
- Emergency contacts are shared
> Save the local emergency number, hotel contact, and @Ops_Team contact before departure.
- Travel insurance is confirmed _(if required)_
## Booking
- Flights are booked
- Hotel is booked and confirmation shared
- Airport transfers/local transportation are arranged
## After Travel
- Expense report and receipts are submitted
- Business travel summary is shared with @Manager
