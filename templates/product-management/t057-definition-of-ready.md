# Definition of Ready (DoR) 📋
-! Acceptance criteria are **written** and `testable`

- Scope is defined and agreed
- Designs attached and approved by @DesignLead

- Technical approach reviewed with @DevTeam
- Edge cases and error scenarios considered
- Testing requirements are defined – see [📖 Testing Guide](testing_guide_url)
- Required documentation updates identified
-! No unresolved questions blocking development :white_check_mark:
