## Define the Target Market
- Identify the key customer segments and industries
- Create Ideal Customer Profiles (ICPs) and personas
- Validate the pain points, needs, and motivations
- Prioritize segments with the strongest product–market fit
## Shape the Value Proposition & Messaging
- Articulate your unique value proposition (UVP)
- Define the core benefits and differentiators
- Develop key messages for each target segment
- Align messaging across marketing, sales, and support
## Prepare the Positioning & Pricing Strategy
- Research competitors and alternative solutions
- Define your product’s positioning
- Decide on your pricing model (subscription, freemium, tiered, etc.)
## Choose the Distribution & Sales Channels
- Select the main go-to-market model (sales-led, product-led, or hybrid)
- Define the key distribution channels (e.g., website, marketplaces, partners)
- Set up processes for lead capture and conversion
- Align the channel strategy with the target audience’s behavior
## Develop the Launch & Marketing Plan
- Set success metrics for the launch (sign-ups, MQLs, revenue, etc.)
- Create the campaign plan (including content, email, ads, social, PR, events)
- Prepare the launch materials (landing pages, visuals, videos, release notes)
- Plan out the launch timeline and internal milestones
## Equip the Customer-Facing Teams
- Create sales enablement materials (pitch decks, battlecards, FAQs)
- Train the sales and support teams on product value, USPs, and messaging
- Align customer success playbooks for onboarding and adoption
- Set up a feedback loop between customer-facing teams and the product marketing team
## Launch & Measure
- Execute the launch campaign across the selected channels
- Monitor the key metrics (activation, conversion, retention, NPS)
- Collect customer feedback and support insights
- Schedule the Day-1, Day-7, and Day-30 retrospective reviews
