# Working with checklists ✅
- Add checklist items, mention @users, add dates due_date, and add [relevant links](https://titanapps.io/)
- [priority="High"][dueDate="due_date"][assignee="assignee_user_id"] Add an assignee, priority, and due date
- Make text **bold**, _italic_, or `emphasized`
- Structure checklists with headers
> # Header 1
> ## Header 2
> ### Header 3
- Change the status of checklist items
- Add details to checklist items
> Learn how to work with checklist details in the [📖 Documentation](https://railsware.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/CHK/pages/90636343/Formatting+Guide)
