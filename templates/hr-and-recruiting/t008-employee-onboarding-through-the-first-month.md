# Employee Onboarding 👋
## 📄 Legal Team Onboarding
- Employee details are verified against the approved offer
-! Employment agreement and NDA are **signed and stored**
> Verify the contract, confidentiality agreement, and other required legal documents.
- Required company policies are shared with the employee
-! Legal onboarding is completed
## 💰 Finance Team Onboarding
- Payroll and tax information is collected and verified
- Employee is added to the payroll system
- Expense and reimbursement policies are shared – [Expense Policy](expense_policy_url)
- Access to the expense management system is granted
-! Finance onboarding is completed
## 💻 Hardware Team Onboarding
- Hardware requirements are confirmed for the employee's role
- Laptop and required accessories are assigned by @IT_Support
- Device is registered in the asset inventory
- Required software and security tools are installed
- Employee confirms all equipment works correctly
-! Hardware setup is completed
## 🔐 Security Team Onboarding
- Security policies are shared – [Security Policy](security_policy_url)
- Required security awareness training is completed
- Password manager and MFA are configured 🔑
- VPN and secure remote access are configured, if required
- Role-based access is reviewed
-! Security onboarding is completed
## 👥 HR Onboarding
- Welcome message and _first-day instructions_ are sent
- Company onboarding guide and [Employee Handbook](employee_handbook_url) are shared
- Company structure, products, and values are introduced
- Communication guidelines and internal processes are reviewed
- Employee is introduced to the manager, team, and @Onboarding_Buddy
- Required company onboarding sessions are completed
-! Company onboarding is completed
## 🚀 Project Onboarding
- Project overview, goals, and priorities are reviewed
- Employee's role and responsibilities are discussed with @Manager
- Access to Jira, documentation, repositories, and project tools is verified
- Project workflows and team processes are reviewed
- Employee is introduced to key project stakeholders
- First tasks and **30-day goals** are agreed with @Manager
-! Project onboarding is completed ✅
