## Contract Review Template for the Legal Team
### Contracting Parties Verification
- Verify the list of involved parties
- Check the registration data from the open registers
> * registration country
> * registration number
> * authorized signatory
- Check that your company is signing on behalf of the right legal entity
> For large companies with multiple entities
### Initial Assessment
- Check if a previous agreement with this vendor already exists
- Identify stakeholders for review or input
### Legal Review – Key Terms
- Check the service scope specified in the contract
- Confirm the jurisdiction and governing law
- Check the dispute resolution/arbitration terms
- Validate the liability clauses
- Review the indemnification terms
- Confirm the intellectual property rights and ownership
- Check the confidentiality and data protection terms
- Validate warranties and representations
- Verify the force majeure clauses
- Check the contract renewal clauses
- Review the term and termination clauses
- Ensure proper signature blocks
### Commercial & Compliance Terms of the Contract
- Verify pricing
- Check the payment terms and invoicing
- Confirm pricing and payment terms with finance or procurement
- Review the penalties
- Check service level obligations and performance criteria
- Verify the service exclusivity clauses (if applicable)
- Review compliance with internal policies (e.g., anti-bribery, ethics, code of conduct)
### Collect and Approve the Edits
- Collect comments and suggested edits
- Finalize redlines and send to the counterparty
### Finalization
- Verify that the agreed changes are reflected in the final draft
- Secure signatures from all parties
- Save a fully executed copy in the contract repository
- Update the information in the contract tracking system
> e.g., Confluence, Airtable database, Google Drive, etc.
- Notify stakeholders about the signed contract
