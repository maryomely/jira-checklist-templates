## Policy Review
- Confirm that the internal control policy is approved
- Check the last review date
- Confirm the policy owner
- Review the policy scope
- Link related procedures and instructions and review them
- Collect evidence of completing the previous steps and document the result in this ticket
## Responsibility Review
- Confirm each control has an owner
- Check backup owners for critical controls
- Review approval responsibilities
- Remove former employees from ownership fields
- Confirm responsible people understand their role
> * Do not silently assign responsibility without notifying the person, this can create misalignment and miscommunication
## Process Review
- Compare the policy with the actual process
- Check whether the required approvals are recorded
- Review whether recurring checks were completed on time
- Identify skipped or unclear steps
- Create tasks for addressing process gaps (missing documentation, uninformed stakeholders, etc.)
## Evidence Review
- List the required evidence for each control
- Add links to documents, tickets, reports, or logs
- Check whether evidence includes dates and timeliness markers
> * Log actions, add dates to screenshots, etc.
- Confirm that the evidence is stored in an accessible location
> * For example, if it’s stored on a closed personal drive, it can’t be easily accessed by auditors or the internal team
- Mark missing evidence as a gap
## Exception Review
- Check whether policy exceptions were documented and have expiration dates
> * For example, it is acceptable if EDR is unresponsive on the laptop of a person who is on vacation, but the exception cannot be indefinite - it should be reviewed and removed on time.
- Review unresolved exceptions
- Assign owners for open gaps
- Add deadlines for remediation
- Record the current status of each fix
## Control Area Verification
- Confirm the periodic user access review was completed and add the link to the evidence
- Confirm that vendor risk assessments were completed for in-scope vendors in the current review period; add the link to the evidence
- Confirm that asset inventory and patching reviews ran against the defined SLA and add the link to the evidence
- Confirm that background screening and security training were completed for in-scope employees and link to the evidence
- Confirm incident response tickets were reviewed for the current period and link to the evidence
- Mark any control area without confirmation or attached evidence as a gap and create a follow-up task
